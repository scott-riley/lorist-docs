# Quick navigation (Go to…)

To quickly navigate between folders and documents, hit the search icon in the sidebar, or press `Cmd-K` (on Mac) or `Ctrl-K` (on Linux or Windows) to open the command palette.

<img src="/quick-nav.webp" alt="Jump to document or folder… window in Lorist" />

## Jumping between folders and documents

Start typing the name of a folder or title of a document. Use the up and down arrow keys to navigate the list, and hit `Return` to navigate directly to the selected item. Alternatively, click an item in the list to navigate directly to it.
