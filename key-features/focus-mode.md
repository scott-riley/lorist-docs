# Focus mode

Focus mode dims everything in your document apart from the currently selected block.

<img src="/public/focus-mode.webp" />

It’s useful for staying focused in longer or more distracting documents, or for folks who struggle with distractions like constantly proofreading and questioning our lives when we should be writing words.

## Toggling focus mode

Use the focus mode button in the top right of the editor, or simply hit `Cmd-/` (`Ctrl-/` on Linux or Windows) to toggle focus mode on or off.
